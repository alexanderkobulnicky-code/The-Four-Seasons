Initial organization

Take your time arranging your first repertoire. When you're satisfied, lock the assignments. Later, existing works may be moved only January 1–7.

Back up your repertoire

Your works are stored in this browser on this device, not on a server. Export a JSON backup regularly, especially before clearing browser data or switching phones. Imports replace the current catalog.

Adding music

New works can be added to any season at any time. The waiting room is for unlimited trial listening; promoting a discovery to a season makes the seasonal rule apply immediately. Already-assigned works stay put until January 1–7, except during your initial setup.

Nothing is tracked when you open YouTube. The app does not count plays, rate music, or judge whether you followed the rules.
